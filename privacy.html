Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 28, 2026

WaveResolutions LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), operating the PracticePing platform, is committed to protecting the privacy of our users and the patients of medical practices that use our services. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information provided directly to us, including:

  • Practice name, contact name, phone number, and address (from trial signup forms)
  • Patient phone numbers and names provided by medical practices for the purpose of sending communications
  • Message delivery and response data

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Send SMS and email review requests and appointment reminders on behalf of medical practices
  • Provide and improve the PracticePing platform
  • Respond to inquiries and support requests

3. SMS Messaging

PracticePing sends text messages to patients on behalf of their medical provider. Message frequency varies by practice. Message and data rates may apply. We do not sell or share mobile phone numbers with third parties for marketing purposes. To opt out of SMS messages, reply STOP to any message. To get help, reply HELP.

4. HIPAA

PracticePing operates as a Business Associate under HIPAA for medical practices that use our platform to communicate with patients. We maintain appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect protected health information (PHI).

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the platform (such as SMS and email delivery providers), and only as required by law.

6. Data Retention

We retain patient communication data for the period required by applicable law and as agreed with each medical practice client.

7. Contact Us

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at info@waveresolutions.com.
